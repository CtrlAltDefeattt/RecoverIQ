# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

RecoverIQ is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous
community. Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- communicate with empathy and focus criticism on ideas and code;
- welcome questions and different levels of experience;
- make claims that are supported by reproducible evidence;
- protect private, security-sensitive, and customer information; and
- accept maintainer decisions made to preserve safety and project scope.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private
information, and other abusive conduct are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the primary maintainer through the
contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities
must instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
participation that violates this policy and may temporarily or permanently ban
participants for inappropriate behavior.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when a person is publicly
representing RecoverIQ.
